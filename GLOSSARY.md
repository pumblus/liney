# Liney

A private, local-first journal app for iPhone and iPad. These terms are the product's shared language for scope, code, and issues.

## Language

### Journal content

**Journal**:
The single private collection of entries on this device.

**Entry**:
A journal record with optional title, ordered blocks, and Entry Info.

**Entry Date**:
The date or date-time of the memory, used to place the entry on the timeline.

**All-day Entry**:
An entry tied to a calendar day instead of a specific time.

**Block**:
An ordered content unit: a plain Text Block or a Photo Group.

**Photo Group**:
Photos selected together and kept as one ordered visual group.

**Photo**:
A copied image with its own capture/place metadata.

### Browsing

**Calendar View**:
A month-by-month way to browse the journal, where each day with entries is shown by one of its photos.
_Avoid_: Month view, diary calendar

**Representative Photo**:
The one photo that shows a day in the Calendar View: the first photo of the day's first entry with any photo, in timeline order.
_Avoid_: Cover photo, day thumbnail

**Day List**:
The entries of one calendar day in timeline rows, opened by tapping that day in the Calendar View.
_Avoid_: Day view, day detail

**Month Header**:
The month name above each month of the Calendar View; tapping it opens Go to Month.

**Go to Month**:
The year and month picker, opened from a Month Header, that scrolls the Calendar View to a chosen year and month.
_Avoid_: Month picker, jump to date, Month in View

### Metadata

**Entry Info**:
Entry-level date and place information used by the timeline and editor.

**Photo Info**:
Capture date and place information stored separately for each photo.

### Import and export

**Day One Import**:
Foreground import of a Day One JSON zip into the single journal.

**Markdown Export**:
A portable zip of Markdown entries and referenced JPEG photos.

### Photo storage

**Orphaned Photo File**:
A copied photo file in the app's photo storage that no Photo refers to.
_Avoid_: Stray photo, leftover photo

**Photo Quarantine**:
The holding place where Orphaned Photo Files wait before they are permanently deleted.
_Avoid_: Trash, recycle bin

### Privacy

**App Lock**:
An optional device-authentication gate protecting visible journal content.

### Windows

**Entry Window**:
A window holding one entry's editor alone, with no timeline; its title is the Entry Date.
_Avoid_: Entry scene, detached editor

**Open in New Window**:
The timeline-row action, or row drag, that opens an entry in its own Entry Window, or brings forward the Entry Window already holding it.
